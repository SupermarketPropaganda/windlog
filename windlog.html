Terms of Service & Aeronautical Disclaimer

Version 2025.1 — Certified Aviation Advisory

Windlog is designed for situational awareness, pre-flight NavLog calculation, and cross-country navigation reference. The pilot in command (PIC) retains final regulatory responsibility for the safe operation of the aircraft under FAR 91.3 / ICAO Annex 2.

1. Flight Planning Precision

All magnetic bearings, ground speeds, and fuel burns computed by Windlog are based on standard atmosphere (ISA) and interpolated forecast winds aloft. Pilots must cross-check calculations against approved Aircraft Flight Manuals (AFM/POH).

2. Data Security & Flight Privacy

Flight itineraries and pilot profiles are stored in this browser. Passwords are hashed. Cloud synchronization and email delivery are not configured.
